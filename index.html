About the Studio

One creative director. One AI engineering partner. Four shipped games.

Pizza Hero Gaming is a one‑person creative studio working with an AI engineering partner. I design the games — mechanics, feel, art direction, what's worth building — and Claude writes the code under my direction. Every title ships web‑first: open a tab, play in seconds, no install, no account.

My favorite kind of game is the one you can pick up in 30 seconds and still be discovering something new an hour in. Athanor: Alchemy Merge is the flagship — a discovery‑based alchemy crafter currently in closed beta on Google Play — and the lineup spans bullet‑heaven, roguelike, and idle RPG. More on the way.
